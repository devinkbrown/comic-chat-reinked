## Objective

After LeaveNotify releases a held button and clears hover, do not let the X11 implicit grab restore hover or emit a second `.up` before the pointer re-enters.

## In scope

1. Track whether the pointer is inside the window. LeaveNotify (non-grab) marks it outside; EnterNotify (non-grab) marks it inside. ButtonPress also marks it inside.
2. Drop MotionNotify while the pointer is outside so grab motion cannot undo `(-1,-1)`.
3. Drop ButtonRelease for a button that is not currently held (already released on leave).
