- Command: `python3 tools/ai_route.py --scope 2 --ambiguity 1 --risk 2 --verification 1 --lanes 1`

## Owner

Linux desktop backends (PR #13): `src/platform/x11.zig`, `src/platform/wayland.zig`, `src/platform/services.zig`.

## Objective

Keep incoming Linux paste/DnD first-class for the remaining 8-bit charsets that already type from the keyboard: Windows-1252 and ISO-8859-3/4/6/8, including their COMPOUND_TEXT designators.

## In scope

1. Decode `windows-1252` / `cp1252` `text/plain` charset MIME on X11 and Wayland.
2. Decode ISO-8859-3/4/6/8 MIME (both common casings on X11).
3. COMPOUND_TEXT `ESC - C` / `ESC - D` / `ESC - G` / `ESC - H`.

## Out of scope

- UI chrome (`src/client/`) and the comic renderer
- Full IME editor, AT-SPI/UIA, dedicated drop Event
- Wayland taskbar urgency, `_NET_WM_SYNC_REQUEST`
- Outgoing source DnD / `xdg_toplevel_drag`
- COMPOUND_TEXT 94ⁿ CJK (optional; do not block)
- `ext-data-control` / `wlr-data-control` (optional; do not block)
- Changing unlabeled invalid-UTF-8 fallback away from Latin-1
