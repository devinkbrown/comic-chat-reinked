## Owner

Linux desktop backends (PR #13): `src/platform/x11.zig`, `src/platform/wayland.zig`, `src/platform/services.zig`.

## Objective

Accept the short IANA/GTK charset aliases (`latin1`, `latin9`, `latin5`, `latin2`, `cyrillic`, `greek`) as receive-only clipboard/XDND MIME on X11, and stop collapsing unmatched Wayland latin MIME to ISO-8859-1.

## In scope

1. Intern X11 `text/plain;charset=latin1` / `latin9` / `latin5` / `latin2` / `cyrillic` / `greek` and rank/decode them like the matching ISO-8859 targets.
2. Add those aliases to the Wayland latin MIME table.
3. `knownTextMime` maps unmatched latin-plain MIME to the charset-correct literal, not always ISO-8859-1.

- KeymapNotify, ISO-8859-10/14/16, KOI8-U
